About Me

Hello! I'm a multidisciplinary creator who expresses through code, music, and interactive experiences. My journey spans software development, music composition, and game design - each discipline informing and enriching the others.

As a developer, I craft clean, efficient code and intuitive user experiences. As a musician, I explore sonic landscapes and emotional storytelling through sound. As a game developer, I combine technical skills with creative vision to build engaging interactive worlds.

I believe that creativity thrives at the intersection of disciplines. Whether I'm debugging code, composing a melody, or designing game mechanics, I'm always seeking that perfect balance of technical precision and artistic expression.
